## 1. The piece

Two user-visible money surfaces that today hide their own terms:

**A. Bounty fee display.** Every place a bounty amount is shown or acted on — post, fund, list, read, claim, submit, accept, dispute, decide, finalize, settlement — should show what the worker will actually receive: gross, the 1% room-pool fee, and net, with the basis ("on released payouts only; a refunded award is returned in full with no fee"). The single server choke point is `_viewBounty(row, roomId)` in `server/bounty-escrow.mjs` (line 1299 at the target SHA): every escrow method returning a bounty returns this view, on both the HTTP routes and the MCP profile. The settlement leg already computes the split in `_sweep` (line 2838): its `payout-released` receipt payload carries `grossAmountMillis` / `feeAmountMillis` / `netAmountMillis`, and the returned `{paid, fee}` — but the pre-settlement views carry no fee fields at all at main (verified: `grep -rn "feeDisclosure|awardFeeBreakdown"` over the archived tree returns **zero hits**).

**B. Fee-credit balance / expiry UX (Demigod trial-fee credits).** `server/fee-credit-ledger.mjs` (178 lines at the F1 pin; read in full at the target SHA) is a pure, record-only, in-memory double-entry ledger:

- `CREDIT_EXPIRY_DAYS = 180` (line 26); `recordTrialFee({clientId, trialTaskId, feeMinor})` stamps `expiresAt = clock() + 180 days` (line 95).
- `creditBalance({clientId})` (line 160) derives the balance from the journal: unexpired, unapplied trial fees only. Expiry test, line 167: `if (nowMs > Date.parse(e.expiresAt)) continue;`
- `applyCreditToPlacement({…, expiresAt?})` (line 105): an explicit `expiresAt` may only shorten the credit (`Math.min`, lines 125–131). Expiry test, line ~135: `if (clock() > Date.parse(effectiveExpiresAt)) throw …credit expired…`. Single-use: one application up to the fee amount; **any remainder is forfeited** (module header) — a fact no surface states.
- Amounts/timestamps are strings (minor units / ISO-8601); credits are capped at fees paid per-fee and in total; double-apply is rejected.

Its only production caller is the `quote` action of `TrialTasks.apply` in `server/trial-task-store.mjs`, reached via the trial-tasks routes in `server/routes/record-rails.mjs` (`POST /api/rooms/{roomId}/trial-tasks`, action `quote`; also list/read/receipt routes). Per quote it builds a **fresh** ledger, records one fee, immediately applies `min(trialFee, placementFee)`, and returns `{recordOnly:true, paymentStatus:'not_configured', hypothetical:true, placement, trial, illustrativeCreditMinor, illustrativeEntries, settlement}`. The credit's `expiresAt` exists only buried inside `illustrativeEntries[].expiresAt`; there is **no** first-class balance, expiry, days-remaining, remainder-forfeited, or status field, and no client code renders any of it (verified: `grep -rn "creditBalance|feeCredit"` over `src/` in the archived tree returns no rendering code; `creditBalance` has no caller outside `tests/demigod-jobs-money.test.js`). The ledger is not persisted — a cross-quote, durable balance does not exist and building one is a schema change (see §4, Stage 2 gate).

Terms at main: `docs/legal/terms.md` has **no** "Credits and bounties" section and `server/legal-store.mjs` has `TERMS_VERSION = "2026-10-02"` (both verified in the archived tree). That gap is exactly what bound PR #5019 closes (§4) — do not re-do it.

## 3. What fantastic means

A poster, worker, or Demigod client can answer three questions from the surface in front of them, in **0 extra clicks / 0 extra requests**, without opening Terms or docs:

1. **"What does the worker get?"** — Every bounty view shows gross / fee / net that sum exactly (`gross = fee + net` at milli precision, floor rule), e.g. award 10 → fee 0.1 → net 9.9; a 0.05-credit award shows fee 0 and says so, rather than promising a 1% deduction that rounds away. The fee line appears **before** the irreversible step (fund, accept), not only after settlement. One sentence, ≤ 40 words, in #5019's probed wording (§4) — no second disclosure shape anywhere (API, MCP, Terms, docs, and any future human panel all render the same `feeDisclosure` object).
2. **"What credit do I have, and until when?"** — Every trial-fee quote response carries a first-class credit block: amount recorded, amount applied in the quote, balance remaining after the quote's application, the exact `expiresAt` timestamp (not a bare date), and the rule in one sentence (≤ 30 words): single-use, remainder forfeited on apply, not usable after the stated instant. Expiry wording precision is the UX translation of the pin: pre-policy-decision copy says "not usable after `<expiresAt>`" — true under strict `>` — and must **not** say "expires on `<date>`" (implies dead from the start of that day/instant) or "valid through `<expiresAt>` inclusive" (asserts the undecided policy).
3. **"Is the number I see the number the ledger computes?"** — 100% of bounty views in a scripted post→fund→claim→submit→accept→settle walk carry `feeDisclosure` whose figures match `_sweep`'s settled `{paid, fee}` to the milli; 100% of quote responses carry an `expiresAt` exactly equal to `recordedAt + 180 days`; the boundary behaviour at T and T+1 ms is demonstrated by tests, not asserted in prose.

Exemplars to match (shape, not visuals): Stripe's payout detail (gross / fee / net as separate line items on the same screen as the total — the fee is never a footnote on another page); a gift-card / store-credit balance line that pairs the amount with its full expiry timestamp on the same line. If an exemplar detail is load-bearing for a design choice, web-check it at build time; none is load-bearing here.
