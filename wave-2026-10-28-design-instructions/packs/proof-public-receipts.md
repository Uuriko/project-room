## 3. What fantastic means

A stranger with no account, arriving from a shared link or a search result, can answer in **30 seconds**: what was asked, who did it, what was checked and by whom (platform, another member, or the worker alone), and what the outcome was — and every word of that answer is true for *that* receipt. Concretely, all of these hold at once:

- **Speed:** anonymous `GET /api/public/receipts?limit=20`, `GET /receipts`, and `GET /receipts/{id}` each answer in **≤1,500 ms** wall on a warm production read (same curl method as §2.6), p95 over 20 probes; no public face exceeds 3 s. List payload stays ≤ ~8 KB at `limit=20` for a typical page.
- **Truthful share layer:** for every receipt in a 20-receipt live sample, og/twitter/meta description, JSON-LD Proof level, badge, and share text agree with that receipt's own `.json` (`state` + `verification` + review) — **0 contradictions** (the E3 test, re-run as acceptance §5.3). A `system_checked` receipt is never described as worker-reported; a rejected receipt's share surfaces carry its outcome.
- **Findability:** the wall offers working controls for the filters that exist (agent, room where linked, outcome/state) plus a text search that actually filters; **zero silently-ignored parameters** on the list route — an unsupported parameter is a 422 naming it, on both the JSON and HTML faces. A stranger can reach a named agent's receipts from the wall in ≤2 clicks without knowing a parameter name.
- **A shop window that shows the product:** the wall's first screen leads with completed, accepted work — the default view is not headed by a rejected or test-probe receipt when accepted receipts exist in the list (curation rule in §4 step 6, honesty constraints there). The count line states the proof mix (e.g. how many of the listed receipts are platform-checked vs hash-only) in the page's own graded vocabulary.
- **Lots resolve or stop being promised:** after #4998 deploys, every publicly named settled lot from a room with a recorded opt-in resolves 200 on `/receipts/<lot>` and `.json`; any lot that still 404s is named, with the operator split (§4 step 1c) recorded against it — no lot silently absent.
- **Cards work:** `og.png` returns 200 image/png for every receipt in the sample, and no error response from any public face carries `Cache-Control: public`.
- **The honesty floor does not move:** names remain "as the room recorded them, not identity-verified"; unsigned remains stated until a signer is actually provisioned; "Record not rechecked" remains until `?verify=1` is run. Fantastic is a *stronger true* surface, never a stronger-sounding one. Exemplar for the graded-vocabulary discipline: this page's own `PROOF_LEVELS` pattern (label + scope in the same string) — match it everywhere; do not invent a bare "Verified ✓" mark (receipt-share.mjs header comment forbids it, on executed proof-ux evidence).

**FORK — John's decision, pending (brief §(a), verified-delivery vocabulary):** whether room-claim `verified` becomes a two-tier vocabulary mirroring public-work's `hash_only` / `system_checked` is **John's**, diagnosed-not-built in `~/workspace/wave-2026-10-27-ut-conversion/charters/verified-delivery.md` (Option A recommended there; stored-row migration, reviewer-replay binding, and the deception-canary suite all hang off it).
