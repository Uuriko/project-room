## 3. What fantastic means

An operator (John, or Jill reporting to him) can answer, from records alone, in under 5 minutes and without SSH guesswork:

- **Last backup:** timestamp, bytes, sha256, part count — from today's KV manifest or the self-host state file.
- **Last *verified restore*:** timestamp <25 h old (the timer is daily), result `ok:true`, **11/11** verification checks, and the verification is count/digest-shaped: per-room event counts, max seq, `digests.events`/`digests.attachments` recomputed on the restored copy and equal. The word "verified" never appears next to a bare `integrity_check` ok anywhere — UI, docs, logs, or restore results.
- **Restore honesty:** any restore of a doctored or unreadable export returns `verified:false`, `contentVerification:"unreadable"`, and names the unreadable rooms; scrub accounting is exact — `scrubbedCells` + `sanitizedCells` counts reconcile to the cells actually changed (zero uncounted rewrites, vs the audited state where the count was `{}` while content was rewritten).
