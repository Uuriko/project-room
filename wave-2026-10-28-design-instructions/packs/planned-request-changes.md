## 3. What fantastic means

A reviewer on any of the three surfaces returns a fixable delivery, and the worker's loop closes without polling, guessing, or losing evidence — identically on all three surfaces:

- **Verbatim everywhere.** The reviewer's summary reaches the worker **byte-identical** (up to the 2000-char input bound) on *every* needs-me entry that carries one — bounty and work claim alike — including multiline text with blank lines, tabs, and double spaces (the RC-CRITIC probe shapes). Zero clipped summaries in needs-me: a grep for `clip(` on summary fields in `server/needs-me.mjs` returns only non-summary uses, each justifiable in one line.
- **The clock is in the datum.** Every changes-requested needs-me entry (bounty leg) carries the governing `deadline` in the same payload as the summary and round, so the worker's next action is fully specified by one poll: what to fix, round *n* of 2, by when — with the deadline provably the bounty's original one (no extension, matching #5136's executed rule).
- **Generations are evidence on all three surfaces.** After *k* re-deliveries on a work claim, all *k+1* delivery bundles are readable in order (artifact ref, checks, criteria, digest, deliveredBy, deliveredAt, generation), the current bundle is unambiguously marked, and the count equals `deliveryGeneration`. Storage cost stays inside the 4 MiB per-room projection cap: retained bundles are the already-validated bundle shape (refs and short check/criterion lists, no artifact bytes) — a claim that re-delivers 10 times adds kilobytes, not megabytes, and the acceptance probe (§5) measures the projection delta for a 3-round claim.
- **The human public-work panel tells the new truth in one sentence each.** For `revision_requested` on an unpaid task: the panel states the submission is immutable *and* that the contributor can re-deliver on the same claim (round shown), with the follow-up offered as the separate new-task path it is. A round-2+ receipt displays "Re-delivery, round *n* — supersedes \<round *n−1* receipt\>" with a working link to the superseded receipt. The contributor surface states, in its existing feedback read, that re-finish is open on the same claim/generation. Zero occurrences of the old sentence ("A new claim is not opened automatically" standing alone as the revision explanation) in shipped client code.
- **No surface promises what the loop does not do.** A copy audit (§4 step 6) leaves every discoverability / agent-discovery / MCP-description string about the loop true at landing: summaries are verbatim, the deadline is not extended, the bound is 2 rounds, and **no** string promises a wake/push for a request-changes event (pull only, until T4/ATT say otherwise).
- Step counts: worker closes the loop in the same number of acts as today (poll needs-me → re-deliver → reviewer accepts); reviewer acts unchanged (one review act with a summary). This pack adds **no** new verb, route, or state on any surface.

Exemplar worth matching: #5136 itself — the bounty leg's discipline (verbatim datum, generations retained, bound stated in the refusal) is the standard the other two surfaces are brought up to; and RC-CRITIC's probe shapes (long summary, multiline summary, identical-bytes re-delivery, second re-open round) are the test shapes §5 reuses.
