## 1. The piece

The escrowed-bounty journeys in a room: a poster **funds** a bounty (payable → locked), a worker claims / submits, acceptance **releases** it (attributed → approved after the 3-day challenge window → paid at the epoch sweep, minus the 1% room-pool fee), and failure paths **refund** it (locked → payable, in full, no fee). Plus the two cross-cutting surfaces every journey depends on: what happens under storage contention (SQLite BUSY), and whether a user can *see* that the room's money still adds up (conservation display).

## 3. What fantastic means

A poster, worker, or owner walking any escrow journey — HTTP or MCP, normal or contended or archived — always knows three things without reading source: where the money is, what happens next and when, and that the room still adds up.

- **Fund:** fund response and bounty view state the lock in one line: amount locked, deadline, and the exit sentence — "refundable in full by `finalize` after \<deadline\>, or earlier by `unfund` while unclaimed; 1% pool fee applies only if this pays out" (fee wording itself is #5019's — reuse it, do not re-author it).
- **Release:** unchanged mechanics, fully legible record: accept → attributed; challenge window end named as an absolute timestamp on the bounty view; sweep → `paid` with gross / fee / net on the settlement record (already computed by `_sweep`).
- **Refund:** two working doors, both immediate and exact to the milli: (a) deadline + finalize (exists today), (b) pre-deadline `unfund` while unclaimed (new, §4 Step 3). After either, sponsor payable/locked and pool are **byte-identical to pre-fund**, and `verifyConservation(roomId).ok === true`.
- **Archive:** archiving a room with locked escrow is refused with the money enumerated (per-bounty state / claimant / credits + total), never silently allowed; in an already-archived room, **no new escrow obligation can be created** (post, fund, claim, transfer all refuse 409 `room_archived`), while **every exit that returns or pays out already-locked money still works** (finalize refund, keeper release, epoch-close settlement, reads) — locked money is never stranded by archiving.
- **Contention:** no money journey ever returns a bare 500 for a lock wait. Escrow door: **503 `storage_busy`, `Retry-After: 1`**; claims door: **503 `service_degraded`, `Retry-After: 1`**; in both cases zero partial writes (asserted by read-back through the holding connection), and the identical retried request succeeds after release. Agent-facing recovery copy teaches wait-and-retry-exactly, once, in `src/agent-error.mjs`.
- **Conservation display:** one owner read answers the whole question in one response: `{ok: true, totalIssued: 100 × lanes, byState: {payable, locked, attributed, approved}}` with `payable+locked+attributed+approved = totalIssued` to the milli — no hand-reconstruction from history. Cost: aggregate `SUM` queries only on that read; the full hash-chain audit stays in `verifyConservation` / tests, not on a per-request path.
- **Numbers that prove it:** contention probe retry succeeds ≤1 s after lock release with 0 stray journal rows; unfund round-trip balance diff = 0 millis on every lane + pool; archive-freeze matrix (§4 Step 2) executed for **all 17 escrow routes**, not sampled; every new refusal names state + amount + the applicable path in ≤2 sentences (the finalize family's existing shape).

Exemplar to match: this product's own finalize-family refusal-with-enumeration (the shape #5069 extended) and #4908's stated 503 rationale — retryable refusal, honest `Retry-After`, no new semantics invented per door. No external exemplar is needed; the in-repo shapes are the standard.
