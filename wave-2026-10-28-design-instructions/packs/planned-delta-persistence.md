## 1. The piece

Stop rewriting the whole room projection on every append. Today, persisting one ~1.1 KB event also serialises and rewrites the entire room state into `rooms.projection`, in the same transaction. Delta persistence changes only the persist step: insert the event row (the `events` table **already is** a per-event delta log — no new delta table, no new schema), update `rooms.sequence` only, and write the full projection only at checkpoint cadence, into `rooms.projection` + `projection_checkpoints`. Reads and rebuilds that miss the warm in-memory cache fold from the last checkpoint + tail events through the store's existing `_replayRoom` machinery.

## 2. Current state

**The defect is executed, twice, by two independent waves.**

- **PERF-50** (`~/workspace/wave-2026-10-27-perf-50/SYNTHESIS.md` §1, §3 #1; lanes B2, F5, J1-a): every write pays one synchronous full commit that rewrites the entire room projection — **~3,700× byte amplification at the then-largest room and rising**; `store.command` p50 62 ms in-process, of which a raw WAL + `synchronous=FULL` commit is 44 ms; post commit measured from the wake side at p50 86 ms @200 msgs → **163 ms @1,000 msgs, growing with room size**; post→delivery latency is >98% commit. All of it queues on the one serial Durable Object thread, so the write tax is also every other room's read latency.
- **DELTA spike** (`~/workspace/wave-2026-10-27-consult-build/lanes/DELTA/REPORT.md`, FILED; synthesis §3): three-arm executed harness over the real `RoomStore` + real reducer, fixture corpus shaped like production. At seed projection 1,136,793 B (≈ production largest at measurement): main writes **1,242,561 logical bytes/write**; the delta prototype writes **1,104 B/write steady-state — 1,125× fewer** (10,445 B amortised over 600 writes with the K=500 checkpoint charged; 47,172 B even at K=100); write-loop p50 21.06 ms → 1.30–1.76 ms in-memory. At 3,612,994 B the ratio reproduces at **3,368×** (1,104 B vs 3,718,762 B). **Byte-equality PASS: 11/11 sha256 checks, 5 corpora, 0 mismatches** — fold-from-checkpoint+tail equals the stored projection byte-for-byte. Caveat carried verbatim: the spike ran in the store's in-memory mode under a write-fault episode — bytes, ratios and hashes are exact; latencies exclude the fsync component, which *understates* delta's win (the 44 ms FULL-commit component is paid on 1.24 MB today, on ~1.1 KB under delta).
- **#5033 does not fix this.** DELTA arm B: at its bound head the write cost is identical to main (1,242,561 B/write) plus a ~0.4% amortised checkpoint tax. #5033 fixes rebuild (206.7 ms from checkpoint vs 3,377 ms full fold at cap scale, 16×) — it is delta's foundation, not its substitute. Anyone who expects the write queue to drain when #5033 lands will be disappointed; that was never its claim.

**What delta does not fix (priced elsewhere, do not smuggle in):** rebuild/fold cost — delta ties or slightly loses there by design; a cold, cache-less read costs a checkpoint parse + tail fold (24–285 ms band at production scale; ~2.8 s for a 500-event tail at 16 MB state) vs a direct projection parse (6–108 ms). The reducer's quadratic tail-fold term is C1-1/C1-2 (reducer Map/light-clone, 10–16 lh, PERF-50) — a sibling build. ## 3. What fantastic means

A post to the largest room costs what the post weighs, not what the room weighs — and every reader, including a cold one, can prove the bytes it folded are the bytes that were written.

Numbers that prove it (all executed at the gate-scale harness and in the named suites, not asserted):

- **Bytes:** steady-state logical bytes/write ≤ ~2 KB at a ≥1.1 MB room (spike: 1,104 B vs 1,242,561 B — ≥1,000×); with the K=500 checkpoint fully amortised, ≤ ~11 KB/write (spike: 10,445 B — ≥100×). The win grows with the room; it must never invert at small rooms (a sub-checkpoint room may simply keep today's behaviour if the implementer proves that cheaper — say which in the PR body).
- **Latency:** in-harness write-loop p50 in the 1.3–3.5 ms class at 1.1–3.6 MB rooms (spike arm C), and end-to-end `store.command` p50 at production-largest scale at most half of the post-#5033 baseline measured in Step 2, with the on-disk commit component now paid on ~1.1 KB. PERF-50's framing: commit 163 ms @1,000 msgs → single-digit class; write ceiling ~3×. Post→delivery p50 follows the commit, as it does today (>98% commit).
- **Correctness, byte-exact:** fold-from-checkpoint+tail == incrementally maintained live state == stored projection at checkpoint, sha256-equal, at every corpus scale tested — the spike's 11/11 standard, re-run at gate scale, plus crash-mid-tail recovery (a process death between event insert and checkpoint loses nothing: the tail is the events table).
- **Bounded staleness, visible:** checkpoint lag (head sequence − checkpoint sequence) is ≤ K=500 by construction and is *readable* — a gauge/health value, not a debugging expedition. No reader ever silently serves a stale projection believing it is current: each §1 SQL-extraction reader is converted, forces a checkpoint, or carries a test proving checkpoint-tolerance.
- **Cap honesty preserved:** the 4 MiB cap is judged on the folded (in-memory) projection size, exactly as today — a room at cap still 409s `pilot_limit`; delta never lets a room grow past cap because its stored bytes look small.
- **Rollback is a flag and a sweep, not a project:** delta off → force a checkpoint for every room (the `rehydrateAllProjections` sweep at store.mjs ~3592 is the shape) → `rooms.projection` is current at head again and the old code path is byte-identical to pre-delta.

Exemplar shape to match: the spike's own prototype (~40 lines over real store internals, DELTA §1) and #5033's cadence machinery — the smallest change to the persist step, reusing machinery that already has byte-equality evidence, rather than a new persistence subsystem.

**Step 3 — The persist change, `command()` path.** In the transaction at store.mjs ~6298: keep `INSERT INTO events`, `INSERT INTO commands`, board sync, `messageWrite.persistEntities()`, redaction and all side effects exactly as they are; change only the rooms update to `UPDATE rooms SET sequence=?, archived_at=? Rollout follows the bodies-at-rest precedent: a store-level enable flag (the `this.bodiesAtRest` pattern) defaulting off, so the landed code is dark until Step 8 enables it; justify any deviation from flag-gating in the PR body.

**Step 4 — Satellite writers, classified one by one.** For each writer site from the Step 1 census — at `aaa749065a` the set is: `appendSideTableEvent` (~3588), `initialize()` (~3709), archive (~5316–5317), redaction commit (~4528), delegate-lapse (~6518) and resume (~6597) appends, repair/backfill sweeps (~2176, 3327–3380, 3584–3601) — write one line in the PR body: **delta** (sequence-only update, same treatment as Step 3) or **force-checkpoint** (full projection write, in the writer's own transaction). Default to force-checkpoint for anything rare, administrative, or repair-shaped (initialize, archive, redaction, sweeps — correctness first, they are not the hot path); delta is for append-frequency writers. Narrow variant: everything except `command()` is force-checkpoint, by definition. No writer may be left unclassified, and no writer may keep silently writing a full projection per append under the full variant without its classification line saying why.

**Step 5 — Read-path conversion.** (i) Store reads: `_readProjection` on a cache miss stops trusting `rooms.projection` past its checkpoint sequence — the miss path becomes checkpoint + tail fold via `_replayRoom` (machinery exists; #4126's corrupt-checkpoint fallback already covers the bad-bytes case), with the parsed-at-sequence recorded so callers that need head either fold to head or say so. (ii) The Step 1 SQL-extraction file set (9 files at `aaa749065a`, §1): per file, convert to fold / force-checkpoint-before-read / or prove checkpoint-tolerant with a named test that pins the tolerance (e.g. a directory listing that is allowed to lag ≤K events must say so in a comment and a test, not by accident). (iii) Snapshot/export/backup/integrity readers: audit against the landed SPILL chain's semantics — a snapshot of an archived room (#4265: live window by default, `fullHistory` explicit) must fold or force-checkpoint before it reads, and a backup taken mid-tail must restore byte-equal (count-verified, §5). Do not change any SPILL behaviour; this step only makes its readers delta-safe.

**Step 6 — Bodies-at-rest interaction.** Skipping `storedProjection` per append also skips its `projection_bodies` side-writes. The fold path must therefore hydrate bodies from the event log — the fallback exists (`_restoreBodiesFromLog`, store.mjs line 3614) — and checkpoint writes continue through `storedProjection` so bodies land at cadence. Parity tests: a bodies-at-rest room read back after (a) pure delta appends, (b) a checkpoint, (c) a crash mid-tail, must be byte-equal to the same room written on the pre-delta path, including the #5116 hot-room (≥1 MiB) selection case.

**Step 7 — Cadence and lag observability (1–2 lh, in the same PR).** Reuse #5033's K=500 and its checkpoint-coverage helper (whatever it is named at the gate head — it is not on `aaa749065a`). Add the C2-2 freshness metric: checkpoint lag per room (head sequence − checkpoint sequence, and the age of the oldest un-checkpointed event) exposed where the storage gauge reads, so the trigger gauges and the lag live in one place. Alert threshold for the operator note in the PR body: lag >2K events sustained is a defect report, not a tuning observation.

**Step 8 — Enablement and landing.** This build changes what `rooms.projection` *means* for every reader in the product and sits in the shared store core that money paths (claim serialisation, conservation) run on: it is **shared-core — it lands singly, green at exact head**, under Rules v2; Ruling 41 tree-binding does not relax it. Fix lanes never merge; the seat lands.
