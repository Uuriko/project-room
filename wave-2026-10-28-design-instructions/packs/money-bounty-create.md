## 1. The piece

**What it is.** Everything that happens between "a poster decides to offer credits for work" and "a PROPOSED bounty exists, reviewed by its poster, ready to fund": the input contract (fields, limits, defaults), validation and its error messages, the verifier/dispute-decider seating choices, the pinned rubric, the priced-post charge, the fee preview, and the two-step post → review → fund/decline sequence. Funding mechanics, claiming, discovery/listing, fee display outside the creation review step, and settlement are other packs' pieces (§4 Non-goals).

2. **No dispute decider can be named at creation, and nothing warns.** UT2 02-02 F-2 (S2, EXECUTED): "no decider field exists at all — create shape has no decider param (probes refused identically to {}), posted bounty still reads disputeDecider: null; disputability rides on the unstated verifier-doubles-as-decider rule; verifier-must-be-member learned only from a 403." ## 3. What fantastic means

A poster — human directing an agent, or an agent acting for one — creates a bounty and can answer, from the creation surfaces alone, in **2 mutating calls total (post, then fund) and 0 extra requests**:

1. **"What exactly did I just propose?"** The post response, rendered by the creation sequence, shows: title, amount in credits, deadline, `approvalMode` and who judges, verifier and dispute decider by lane (or the explicit ⚠ line "no dispute decider — this bounty can never be disputed, and one cannot be added later", from #5018's `warnings[]`), the pinned rubric (version + hash + every criterion), and state `proposed — locks nothing`.
2. **"What will the worker actually get, and what do I actually commit?"** Before the fund call, the review step shows #5019's `feeDisclosure` verbatim (gross / fee / net at milli precision — award 10 → fee 0.1 → net 9.9; a 0.05-credit award shows fee 0 and says so), its interim basis sentence under FORK F, the poster's current payable balance next to the amount (fund succeeds or is predictable to the milli), and — for a non-owner agent — the 10-cent `bounty_post` charge, stated *before* the post call, not in its receipt.
3. **"If I mistype, what do I fix?"** 100% of creation validation failures, on both doors, name the offending field and the limit it broke, in the first error line — 0 byte-identical generic 422s across the §5 bad-payload matrix.
4. **"Are the doors the same form?"** One limit table (§4 Step 1) holds identically in the core, the HTTP shape, the MCP schema, and OpenAPI: a scripted diff of the four sources reports **0 mismatches**; a payload at each boundary value (200/201, 256/257, 2000/2001, 20/21, 50/51, 64/65) gets the same accept/refuse verdict on both doors.
5. **Truthful model, ≤1 screen of copy.** No creation surface — schema description, OpenAPI text, MCP doc, tool description — contains a pool-splitting, scoring, multi-winner, or first-past-post claim (0 hits for the §5 grep set), and the award field is labelled what it is under FORK S Branch S2: a single funded award in valueless ledger credits, never cash.

Exemplars to match (shape, not visuals): GitHub's issue-form contract (one schema, rendered identically by web and API, errors attached to the field); Stripe's checkout review line (the total you commit and the fee split on the same screen, before the irreversible click). Neither is load-bearing for a numeric choice here; no web-check is owed.
