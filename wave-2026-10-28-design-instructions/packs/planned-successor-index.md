## 1. The piece

**What it is.** A signed, digest-only, append-only **public index of settled receipt bundles**: one entry sequence (settlement entries + status records in the *same* sequence), periodically sealed by signed, RFC 3161-timestamped **checkpoint** records committing to a Merkle root over the covered entries, plus **bundle retrieval-by-digest** and an **offline verifier** a sceptic runs against a held bundle + a held index copy with no platform serving involved. Presence in the index is witnessed proof a settlement happened; **absence is neutral, forever** — it is never a validity claim, a licence registry, or evidence a private room did no work (lane 15 §8.3 #4).

**It is not** the existing public receipts wall. The wall (`/receipts`, `/api/public/receipts`) is a read-time list over a derived mirror table; the successor index is a separate signed artefact, written once at settlement finality, never derived at read time from any other store (lane 15 §3.1).

## 3. What fantastic means

A stranger who trusts nothing we serve can, offline, prove a settlement happened — and anyone truncating or forging the index is caught by construction:

- **Completeness is witnessed, not asserted.** Every checkpoint carries `entry_count == covered_entry_seq` as the ExpectedLength witness and a Merkle `entries_root`; a copy missing one covered entry fails step 3 as forged/truncated — never downgrades to "unanchored". (F1 gate, §5.)
- **Status is authenticated.** A `revoked`/`superseded`/`disputed`/`withdrawn` record moves a verdict only if its digest recomputes, it is a Merkle leaf under the covering checkpoint, and its actor key was authorised for that act at the witnessed time; anything else renders *ignored, with the reason*. (F2 gate, §5.)
- **Verification is 7 steps, offline, zero platform calls** (lane 15 §5): bundle↔entry binding, inclusion, anchoring, witnessed time (asserted vs witnessed never conflated), identity pinning against the published key history, chain continuity to genesis, status — each independently reportable, none collapsed into one "verified" badge.
- **Cadence is auditable from the artefact itself:** checkpoint at least every **24 h** and every **100 entries**, whichever first; **immediately** on any `revoked` status record or any key-history rotation/revocation; genesis checkpoint 0 commits to the empty index. Lateness is visible in the record (`generated_at` vs TSA time).
- **Privacy is a closed field set:** entries/checkpoints/status carry digests, seqs, bands and fingerprints only — zero room content, zero names/seat ids, zero exact amounts, zero free text, zero digests of guessable raw values (lane 15 §3.3's 8 never-appear rules). `amount_band` (coarse enum) is the maximum money information, and only if the room's opt-in granularity includes bands.
- **Numbers to hit:** tamper battery 100% safe verdicts (C4 cases R, 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 6b + 5b partial resolved to FAIL-or-neutral-with-reason, never silent pass); lane 15 §9's ten probes 10/10 executed-pass; never-appear audit 0 extra fields / 0 grep hits; private-vs-fabricated digest lookups indistinguishable in shape on every surface; mirror-halt verification (probe 8) passes from a participant-held copy alone.
- **Exemplar posture:** a transparency-log witness in the RFC 6962 / SCITT-Rekor shape lane 15 §8–§10 points at — signed checkpoints, fork = self-contained equivocation evidence any two mirror holders can demonstrate without trusting either copy.
