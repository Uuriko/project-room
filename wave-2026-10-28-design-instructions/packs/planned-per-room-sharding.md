## 1. The piece

Replace the single-writer topology — every room in one Durable Object — with one Durable Object per room (`ROOM_SHARD.getByName(roomId)`), leaving `invite-only-pilot` as the directory / global object. A room is a serial log and stays serial *inside* its shard; what sharding removes is one room queueing behind every other room, plus cron / integrity / patrol sharing the same thread.

## 2. Current state

**The ceiling is measured, not hypothesised.** PERF-50 SYNTHESIS §1 / §5 and lane J5 (on J4's executed load test): one DO = one serial writer for the whole product; write-heavy throughput collapses **114 → 11.5 rps between VU 10 and VU 25 (p50 62 ms → 1.8 s, zero errors — pure queueing)**; read-mix saturates ~125 rps. Burst headroom today is ~2–4×, not 10×. J5's 100× basis: ~500–1,000 writes/s bursts and ~55,900 rooms against a measured-class ceiling of ~50–125/s — no tuning closes that gap; only topology does.

## 3. What fantastic means

A member or agent in a busy room cannot tell that any other room exists, let alone that it is busy — and an operator can prove every migrated room's bytes are exactly where its `storage_home` says they are.

Numbers that prove it (all measured at the composed head / in production telemetry, not asserted):

- **Isolation under the J4 shape:** re-run the PERF-50 J4 write-heavy load shape with load split across ≥10 rooms: aggregate write throughput does **not** invert between VU 10 and VU 25 (baseline failure: 114 → 11.5 rps); cross-room interference is nil within measurement noise — a hot room's burst moves another room's post p95 by <10%.
- **Per-room honesty preserved:** a single hot room still serialises internally (correct — it is a serial log); its post-commit latency is no worse than the same room pre-sharding at the same size, with delta persistence landed (post commit O(message), single-digit-ms class, not whole-projection).
- **Production gate numbers stay green after cutover:** burst write p95 < 1 s at the load that fired the trigger; `/api/ready` answers from the correct home for pilot and sharded rooms; zero 404s from `RoomShard.fetch` for a room whose `storage_home = "room"` (the stub's unconditional 404 is gone for routed rooms, and remains impossible to reach for unrouted ones).
- **Migration proof per room:** `compareRoomHomes` returns `same: true` (events, eventsHash, messages, projectionBytes) at flip and at every soak check; a room slice from `exportRoomNdjson` restores via `restoreRoomNdjson` with strict audit green and event/row **counts** verified against a manifest (count-verification certifies restores — Discovery-50 F3 — an `integrity ok` alone does not).
- **Money invariants untouched:** per-room serial logs preserve serialised-by-construction claim handling and conservation exactly (money-core invariants 1–16, execution-graded HOLD 2026-10-25); cross-room money movement stays on the existing keeper/escrow rails — zero new cross-room money paths.
- **Rollback is a sentence, not a project:** before any prune, rollback = unset the flag + set `storage_home` back to `pilot`, reads already servable from the pilot because pilot rows were never deleted. After a prune (separately gated, §4 Step 7), rollback = restore from the verified room backup — and the pack says so out loud wherever prune is mentioned.

Exemplar shape to match: the runbook already in the tree (`docs/design/ROOM-SHARD-CUTOVER.md`) — dark deploy first, one named internal room, compare-or-stop at every step, each irreversible act behind its own named go. Extend that discipline; do not replace it with a big-bang cutover.

**Step 3 — Route in the Worker.** `cloudflare/room.mjs`: the fetch forward, ready probe, job-health, and scheduled paths stop naming `'invite-only-pilot'` literally for room-scoped requests and consult `resolveRoomHome(env, roomId, db)`. **Step 4 — Dual-write on the command path.** Wire `mirrorRoomIfEnabled` (or its successor) into `store.command` for rooms with `storage_home` in transition, so a copied shard cannot silently go stale; a mirror failure must surface, not swallow (the pilot remains authoritative until read flip). **Step 5 — Jobs, integrity, patrol, backup per topology.** Decide and write down, per background function, whether it is pilot-wide, per-shard, or fanned out: alarms/scheduled fast jobs, integrity job, patrol, store-growth, nightly backup. Pilot-wide operational tables stay pilot-wide and are classified `skipped`/directory for shard copy (never copy another room's queue into a shard). Backup becomes: nightly pilot NDJSON (directory + unmigrated rooms) **plus** per-shard room slices with count-verified manifests; restore drills run per topology. Re-scope SPILL per shard here (spill becomes per-room — J5 notes this is easier, but it is a re-scope, not an assumption) and the #3585-remainder export becomes per-shard export. Do not replay a full pilot export through `restoreRoomNdjson`, and do not add a REPLAY skip to make a restore pass — an unclassified new table is classified in `room-shard-tables.mjs` in the same change that creates it, or backup fails closed.

**Step 6 — Migrate by the runbook, amplified.** Follow `docs/design/ROOM-SHARD-CUTOVER.md`'s sequence per room, with the new Steps 2–4 machinery replacing its manual tail: (1) flag on in the named deploy (John's go); (2) `ensureStorageHomeIfEnabled` on the pilot (this is the schema change — existing rows read `pilot`); (3) copy **one named internal room**; (4) `compareRoomHomes` must return `same: true` or stop — do not set `storage_home`; (5) dual-write live (Step 4) instead of repeated manual copies; digest must stay `same: true` under live traffic for a named soak window; (6) read flip for that room only (separate go per the runbook); (7) soak, then cohorts of rooms by size ascending, largest/hottest room last and alone. Rollback at every point before Step 7 = flag off + `storage_home` back to `pilot`.

**Step 7 — Prune (separate charter, separate go — do not bundle).** Pilot rows for a migrated room are deleted only after: two verified room backups, a green strict-audit restore of the slice, count-verified manifest, and John's explicit go for that room/cohort. The runbook's warning is binding: prune does not switch any audit to `"report"` to make a red strict audit look green, and after prune, rollback is restore — say so in the PR body.

**Step 8 — Landing discipline.** Split into singly-landing PRs by phase (schema/flag-column · RoomShard serving · routing · dual-write · jobs/backup · each is money-adjacent or shared-core until proven otherwise); every PR `ready-to-land`-labelled for heavy CI, green at exact head, Ruling 41 evidence binding below the money line does **not** apply to the schema/routing/dual-write PRs (they are at/above it). Fix lanes never merge; the seat lands. Deploys are Codex's release seat from John's Mac, with John's go per runbook act (flag set, production copy, read serve, prune are four separate goes, not one).

**Explicit non-goals:** flipping `ROOM_PER_ROOM_OBJECTS` as "the sharding work" (C-2 — it arms a 404); raising the 4 MiB projection cap as a substitute (runbook: cap unchanged, a capped room still 409s `pilot_limit`; sharding is not a way around it); the typed-object redesign before or inside this programme; edge read models, queue-based wake, and search split (sibling 100× phases with their own triggers in §2's table — their triggers firing starts *their* packs, and only re-opens this pack's Step 0 review if the firing trigger is burst-p95 or room count); any cross-room money redesign; any stack/vendor change (Cloudflare-native only, per J5); moving any DIRECTORY_TABLE into a shard without a written classification amendment.
