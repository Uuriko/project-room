## 3. What fantastic means

A sender, a coordinator, and the woken seat can each read the same server facts, and the ladder is the ordinary path — not Jill's private bridge:

- **Every mention of a hosted seat produces a persisted wake item in Queued**, addressed under the recorded D8 substrate (Step 2 charter §1.1), carrying the Step 1 carried idempotency key. No persisted row ⇒ no stage is ever claimed for that wake; fallback wakes are reported as fallback wakes with no item.
- **Stages are server records, not consumer journals:** Claimed (Step 1 claim door, 60 s lease), Resumed (a resume record bound to the claim's fencing token, carrying the session-log cursor resumed from), TurnStarted (a turn record bound to the item's idempotency key; one key ⇒ at most one live turn), Settled (a retrievable settlement record carrying terminal outcome + a result pointer that resolves + settlement time, readable fresh after every stream disconnects). A bare ack is labelled legacy-ack and counts toward no stage, ever.
- **Liveness is honest under failure:** claim heartbeat every 20 s (TTL ÷ 3), independent of the 120 s presence heartbeat; a claimer that has not reached TurnStarted within 10 min of renewals releases or loses the claim at next expiry and the item returns to Queued with `redelivered` set; a turn exceeding its 1 h budget goes to `unknown` — never auto-failed, never auto-succeeded.
- **The completion ledger (Step 3) answers in one request:** per-stage counts and ages, which claims are waiting on which escalation asks and aging how, dead-letter stock (visible, in zero claimable/pending sets), and the fencing journal — with every count derived from stage/ledger records only, never from a serve counter, and disagreements reported as named gaps, not reconciled by invention.
- **Numbers that prove it:** SPEC proof **P7 passes twice** from cold start (Step 2's done condition); mention→Claimed on the healthy path stays at the bridge's proven shape (p50 ≤ 2 s, the E5 C6 budget shape) *with the claim recorded server-side*; the post-landing discharge re-measurement (§5) shows wakes-path 429 share → ~0 and zero unexplained HTTP 500/503 in the comparable window; zero serves named `delivered` in any served vocabulary, counter leg, or evidence pack.
- **Exemplar discipline:** the session-consumer contract's one architectural rule — the durable signal is the payload, every channel is a hint, reconciliation on (re)start is mandatory. This pack does **not** promise to wake a closed app; for a session seat the fantastic state is "queued, counted, oldest-age visible, reconciled on return," stated on the health/ledger read instead of discovered by forensic reconstruction.
