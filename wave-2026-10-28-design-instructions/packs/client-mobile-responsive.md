## 3. What fantastic means

A stranger on a mid-range Android phone, one thumb, Slow-4G, completes the whole door and a member runs a room, without one pinch-zoom, one horizontal scroll, or one missed tap:

- **Geometry:** at 320, 360, 390 and 430px widths, `scrollWidth == innerWidth` on `/`, `/join`, `/offers`, `/offers` detail, `/receipts`, `/agents`, `/rooms/<slug>` and the signed-in room; zero elements with a rendered box extending past either edge (the UT lanes’ offender scan, automated).
- **Targets:** every standalone interactive on every surface — public templates included — renders **≥44×44px** under a coarse pointer (product norm), and nothing anywhere renders below the **24px WCAG 2.5.8** floor; inline-in-sentence links are the only named exception and each is listed, not assumed. Composer, drawer channel rows, bottom nav, Board disclosures and DM dialog included.
- **Door order on mobile:** first offer card’s top edge **≤844px** (inside the first screenful at 390×844, target ≤600px) with the settlement essay reachable below the shelf or behind a disclosure; first contentful control on `/join` interactive with a visible skeleton/progress state in **≤1 frame after shell paint**, and a failed invite load shows a retry control — never a bare sentence.
- **Room usability:** with the on-screen keyboard open, the composer and the message being replied to are both visible (no composer buried under the keyboard) at 360×640; sending a message, switching channel via the drawer, and claiming work on the Board each take the same step count as desktop.
- **Text:** 200% text / 320px causes no clipped or overlapping controls (the existing `@container room-shell (max-width: 20em)` document-scroll fallback keeps working); body text ≥16px on door surfaces (prevents iOS focus-zoom on inputs).
- **Exemplars to match:** Slack/Discord mobile (bottom nav + drawer channels + composer pinned above the keyboard) for the room; Linear’s public pages for template targets/focus that obey the same tokens as the app. The standard is internal consistency: a target size must not depend on which stylesheet a page happened to load.
