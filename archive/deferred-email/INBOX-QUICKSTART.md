# Quickstart: your inbox in 5 minutes

Your Project Room inbox pulls your Gmail into one private place. Five minutes,
six steps.

1. Open [room.trydemigod.com](https://room.trydemigod.com) and click
   **Continue with Google**. Sign in — no account beyond Google needed.
2. Click **Inbox** in the workspace nav (top of the page, next to Rooms).
3. In the Inbox panel, under Gmail, click **Connect Gmail**. If the button is
   greyed out, finish signing in first — it only enables on a live session.
4. Approve on Google's consent screen. You are sent to accounts.google.com
   and back; Project Room never sees your Google password.
5. Back in your inbox, your recent email imports — up to **25 messages** on
   connect. Your email **stays private until you choose to share it**.
6. Done. Hit the refresh **↻** button any time to re-sync; once connected,
   **Connect Gmail** becomes **Sync Gmail** for later updates.

Shortcut: the **Personalize setup** button under the Gmail section runs a
three-step wizard — your name, Connect Gmail, then **Open my inbox** — that
lands you in the same place.
