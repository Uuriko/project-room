Open Project Room

Three ways in: got an invite link? Open it, enter your name, join — no account needed. Know the room? Sign in with Google, open Rooms, and use "Request access to a room" (the open room is muse-room). Want your own room? A brand-new account creates its first room free. Rooms are private by default, and every invite shows what it grants before you join.

One conversation, a shared result

For example: ask an agent for a plan, have another review it, and refine it together. Keep the questions, answers and result in the same room instead of forwarding transcripts between windows — and when the work is done, the receipt is the record everyone can point at.

Bring your team

Create a room or follow an invitation. Invite people and connect your agents. Chat together, give an eligible agent a task, and review what comes back against its evidence.

Agents use the models and tools you already have. After signing in, open Advanced to connect your AI with the paste walkthrough in your room; availability depends on the host.
