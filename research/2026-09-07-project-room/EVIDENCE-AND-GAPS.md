# Evidence and gap ledger

7 September 2026. Internal research record, not a systematic literature review.

## First-wave synthesis (before second-wave retrieval)

The initial evidence favors a collaboration product that can acquire marketplace capabilities incrementally. The value hypothesis is continuity and dependable review, not agent population or task volume. Mercor-style repeat expert work is an optional later mode. One work record remains useful, but a universal identical form or identical legal policy does not follow. Physical tasks and pooled rewards have materially different commitments.

The legal lane overturns the assumption that work swaps are necessarily a simpler paid-pilot substitute. The technical lane overturns the assumption that protocol task completion and cancellation mean business success or stopped execution. The UX lane qualifies 'no words': unfamiliar actions and consequential commitments need understandable labels and terms. Human-AI studies support task-specific augmentation, not automatic synergy; agent-simulation evidence does not establish human usability or demand.

## Initial primary sources reviewed by root

- Vaccaro, Almaatouq and Malone, Nature Human Behaviour, 28 October 2024: https://www.nature.com/articles/s41562-024-02024-1 . Read abstract, results and methodology context. 106 experiments/370 effects from 2020-June 2023. Combined systems beat humans alone on average, but not the better standalone baseline; creation subgroup estimate alone was not significantly above zero. Historical heterogeneous evidence, not a modern universal effect.
- Amershi et al., CHI 2019: https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/research/wp-content/uploads/2019/01/Guidelines-for-Human-AI-Interaction-camera-ready.pdf . Read abstract and guideline table/method context. 18 guidelines, practitioner evaluation; supports correction, capability boundaries and contextual controls, not proof of this UI.
- Brynjolfsson, Li and Raymond, QJE, published 4 February 2025: https://academic.oup.com/qje/article/140/2/889/7990658 . Read original abstract, rollout design, heterogeneity and caveats. 5,172 support workers at one firm; staggered adoption study, not generic randomized proof for all agent tasks.
- METR, 24 February 2026: https://metr.org/blog/2026-02-24-uplift-update/ . Read original update. Newer productivity estimates are unreliable for effect size because of participant/task selection and concurrent-agent time measurement; do not recycle early-2025 slowdown as a current universal claim.
- Cemri et al., arXiv 2503.13657v3, 26 October 2025: https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.13657 . Abstract/version history reviewed; taxonomy diagnoses specification, coordination and verification failure, not an estimate of Project Room reliability.
- Yao et al., UXAgent, arXiv 2504.09407v1, April 2025: https://arxiv.org/html/2504.09407v1 . Read abstract and limitations/discussion. Evaluates researchers' qualitative perspectives, not validated equivalence between simulated and human shopping behavior.
- NN/g, Progressive Disclosure, 3 December 2006: https://www.nngroup.com/articles/progressive-disclosure/ . Principle and disclosure criteria reviewed. Two-level discoverability and task-relevant primary information; practitioner guidance, not effect-size evidence.
- NN/g, Icon Usability, 27 July 2014: https://www.nngroup.com/articles/icon-usability/ . Primary research/practitioner article; unfamiliar icon meanings need context and labels.
- W3C WCAG 2.2 Understanding: https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/status-messages.html ; https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/target-size-minimum.html ; https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/accessible-authentication-minimum.html . Current technical guidance, checked September 7. 24 CSS pixel target criterion has exceptions; status changes should be available without unnecessary focus interruption.
- Apple HIG accessibility was attempted but returned only a JavaScript shell. No detailed claim is based on its inaccessible text.

## Second-wave resolution

- Incumbent challenge: current Linear delegation and GitHub Agent HQ already support human-owned, multi-provider work. Continuity is not a unique differentiator. Test a concrete cross-environment result/acceptance job against existing tools.
- Review economics: crowd-contest evidence is conditional on uncertainty, not a universal case for competition. Separate paid independent review should be risk-triggered, not imposed on every tiny item. Pay for bounded diagnosis when completion scope is unknown.
- Barter: current IRS instructions do not require credits for barter-exchange status. Bilateral matching is not an established safe harbor. Voluntary collaboration, explicit swaps and transferable credits are distinct offerings.
- Physical tasks: Taskrabbit terms/expense policy demonstrate distinct permission, qualification, expense, custody and home-entry issues. Digital first; exterior observations, pickups and in-home work require separate release decisions.
- Privacy/payment clocks: Upwork supplies a concrete review-window pattern, not a universal legal deadline. FTC guidance supports minimized records. Keep required agreements and adjudication evidence, not blanket four-year trace retention.
- Dasha local source: missing usage is coerced to zero, local coordinator jobs are ephemeral, and provider cancellation fixtures do not prove hosted stop. The hosted implementation is elsewhere; capability is unresolved, not proven absent. Owner-confirmed idempotency/status/cancel/metering contract remains a gate.
- MCP contradiction: direct official releases page confirms July 28 stable2026-07-28 at5f5440b. The stale search snippet described May29 RC. Actual host compatibility remains untested.
- Evaluation: original judge paper supports position/verbosity limitations but explicitly cannot establish self-enhancement conclusively. Avoid the stronger claim. AI Agents That Matter supports joint cost/accuracy and holdouts; paper HTML failed once, accessible original abstract used for these limited claims.
- Reciprocity: 2016 manipulated past-help signals; upstream test relied on historical proxies. 2026 Stack Overflow working paper uses observational matching/DiD with remaining confounding. Different mechanisms; no guaranteed pay-it-forward.
