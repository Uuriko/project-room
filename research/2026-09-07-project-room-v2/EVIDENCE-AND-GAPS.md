## First synthesis before follow-up

Room's likely opportunity is dependable collaboration around a small shared agreement, not an agent directory or new eval engine. Request preparation, explicit handoff changes, result evidence and fair closeout should be one quiet flow. Cash is an optional contractual layer with its own financial state. A useful decline can be a successful contribution. None of this establishes demand.

## Follow-up conclusions and stop decision

- Vasconcelos et al., CSCW2023, arXiv2212.06823v2: cheaper verification can reduce overreliance. Five maze studies, simulated AI and intentionally ideal explanations; not high-stakes/open-ended task proof. Reconciles rather than overturns Bucinca2021. Root inspected methods, discussion and limitations.
- Park et al., arXiv2411.10109v3 June2026: interview-grounded agents outperform invented personas on some held-out survey responses; 65.67% raw GSS accuracy, 83% normalized to human retest consistency. Not 83% absolute and not a usability trial. Root independently checked denominators. Bisbee et al. Political Analysis2024: synthetic averages can mask invalid variation/relationships, historical models and political surveys limit transfer.
- Stripe funds-segregation preview is separately enabled/per-payment; ordinary metadata and Room allocation are not financial segregation. Its supported-method/geography limits do not match a universal fiat/stablecoin route. Changing eligibility must be operation-specific/current, not an enduring onboarding badge.
- Collision sources: Burrows2006 Chubby; etcd3.6; GitHub merge queues; EC2 idempotency contract. Root independently inspected Chubby original and Paperclip checkout docs. Claims cannot stop external agents. Current Room acquireClaim guards the selected work item's claim and records repo/ref/paths; this is not evidence of a cross-work resource arbiter or external execution fence. Existing writer-fence tests concern old database-writer versions, not agent ownership generations.
- Product docs covered Paperclip, Linear, Algora, LaborX, Relay.app, Gumloop and n8n. Root public browser walkthroughs: first five excluding LaborX, including Gumloop. 15 PNGs saved.
