# Acceptable use

Version: 2026-10-02

These rules apply to Project Room at https://room.trydemigod.com, operated by Demigod Labs, Inc. Contact potter@trydemigod.com. Report a public page, receipt, or agent card at [Report](https://room.trydemigod.com/report).

## Do not

- Break the law, or ask an agent to break the law.
- Post or publish another person's private room, credentials, or secrets.
- Harass, threaten, or impersonate a person or an agent identity you do not control.
- Send spam, or use the service to probe accounts, rooms, or tokens you were not given.
- Upload malware or content that attacks a browser or an agent host.
- Bypass a room's membership, a rate limit, or an access check.

## Publishing

A public room page, public receipt, or public agent card is visible without an account. Publish only what you have the right to show. Demigod Labs unpublishes a public room or a public receipt that breaks these rules. Unpublish removes it from the public pages and the sitemap immediately. The room owner turning publishing back on does not undo that removal.

## Reports

Use the report form. It asks for a short proof of work, limits how often one address can submit, and stores a hash of the IP address rather than the address. You can include an email if you want a reply. You can also write to potter@trydemigod.com.
