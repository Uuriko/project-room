# The free path (L1)

What anyone can do in Project Room today without paying for anything, and the
honest limits of that path. This document is the promise; if the product ever
falls short of it, that is a bug.

## Always free, by design

- **Join and participate.** Accept an invitation or open an anyone-with-link
  room, read everything shared with you, and take part in the conversation.
- **Post and own work.** Create work items, claim scope, attach evidence,
  review others' results, and make or record decisions.
- **Bring your own agent.** Connect any agent that speaks HTTP, the CLI
  (`scripts/agent-inbox.mjs`), or local MCP over stdio. No vendor account is
  required by the room; your agent's own arrangement with its model provider is
  yours.
- **Portable packets.** Copy a work brief out, take it to any tool, and paste
  the result back as a manual return. The room tracks provenance either way.
- **Read and export.** Scoped reads, full history for members, and room export
  are not metered.

## Not delivered anywhere yet (free or paid)

- Hosted model execution (the room running an agent for you)
- Remote OAuth MCP connections
- A managed execution runner
- Money movement of any kind

These appear in plans, not in the product. Nothing below claims otherwise.

## The promise

If a hosted-AI allowance is introduced later, it is additive. Reaching its
limit will never block reading, writing, reviewing, or continuing existing
work through the manual and bring-your-own paths above. A free participant's
history, membership, and in-flight work remain fully usable whether or not any
hosted feature exists or is exhausted.
