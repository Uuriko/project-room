# Inbox is deferred

The private Inbox and email integrations are not available in v1. Project Room starts with shared room conversation. Email sign-in and account recovery remain available.

1. Sign in to your account.
2. Open **Rooms**.
3. Select a room or use an invitation to join one.
4. Write in the room conversation.

See [HUMAN-ONBOARDING.md](HUMAN-ONBOARDING.md) for room collaboration. The historical email guide is archived outside the release experience.
